Two axes

Syntax highlighting writes a foreground; a diff writes a background. They compose on one cell grid. See the decision record.

─── Rules

• Measure in display cells, never in bytes.
• Degrade at the gate, never at the call site.
  ◦ A nested claim, wrapped so the continuation has to keep its column.
